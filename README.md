Profitlens
A PHP + MySQL Web App that small business owners can use to track profits, expenses, products and export financial reports all from one admin dashboard.

Characteristics
Dashboard- real-time view of sales, expenses, and net profit
Product Management- add/edit products with inventory tracking
Sales Recording- record sales, auto deduction of stock, monthly profit tracking
Track Business Expenses Organise & Manage
Financial Reports- Charts of Profit & Loss
Revenue Insights- year over year revenue comparison and top products
Excel export- download reports as XLS files
